Process

A clear, three-step engagement.

We keep the process straightforward: understand your environment, redesign with security in mind, and support you as your business grows.

Step 1

Network assessment

We review your existing setup, map devices, identify risks, and document how things are configured today — including ISP gear.

Step 2

Secure redesign

We propose a clear network layout with VLANs, firewall rules, and Wi-Fi configuration tailored to the way your business actually runs.

Step 3

Implementation & handoff

We implement changes, verify performance, and leave you with clean documentation and optional ongoing support if you want it.

Cybersecurity

Practical security for real-world networks.

No scare tactics, no overkill tools — just solid protections that keep POS systems, staff devices, and guest traffic safely separated.

Segmentation & access control

Limit the blast radius when something goes wrong.

  • Dedicated POS & office networks
  • Guest Wi-Fi with internet-only access
  • Separate IoT, cameras, and “risky” devices

DNS & threat reduction

Reduce risky domains and known-bad destinations at the network edge.

  • Secure DNS resolvers
  • Content & threat filtering options
  • Safer browsing for staff and shared PCs

Visibility & logging (optional)

For businesses that want more insight into their network behavior.

  • Basic logging strategies
  • High-level alerting for key systems
  • Guidance on future monitoring tools
