Services

Small business networking, done right.

From slow Wi-Fi and unstable POS systems to confusing “mystery” gear, we rebuild your environment with a clear, security-first design.

Network design & cleanup

We organize and rebuild your network so everything is stable, documented, and easy to support.

  • Router, switch, and Wi-Fi layout
  • Structured VLAN design
  • Clear IP, DHCP, and DNS plan

Firewall & security hardening

Practical protections that fit your tools, your staff, and your budget.

  • Firewall policy review & cleanup
  • Segmentation for POS, office, guest, IoT
  • Secure DNS & safer remote access

Wi-Fi & performance tuning

Eliminate dead zones, dropouts, and random slowdowns across your business.

  • Access point placement & channels
  • SSID and security configuration
  • Bandwidth & QoS adjustments
Cybersecurity

Practical security for real-world networks.

No scare tactics, no overkill tools — just solid protections that keep POS systems, staff devices, and guest traffic safely separated.

Segmentation & access control

Limit the blast radius when something goes wrong.

  • Dedicated POS & office networks
  • Guest Wi-Fi with internet-only access
  • Separate IoT, cameras, and “risky” devices

DNS & threat reduction

Reduce risky domains and known-bad destinations at the network edge.

  • Secure DNS resolvers
  • Content & threat filtering options
  • Safer browsing for staff and shared PCs

Visibility & logging (optional)

For businesses that want more insight into their network behavior.

  • Basic logging strategies
  • High-level alerting for key systems
  • Guidance on future monitoring tools
